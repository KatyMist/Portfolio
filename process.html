Development Process

My main focus is frontend development and working within a team. The stages below show how the work can be organized when building a custom website.

01. Brief & Concept

The work starts with a short brief, where you describe your project, wishes, and preferences.

Based on it, I create a preliminary layout of the future website. We then discuss the proposed concept together, adjust it if needed, and only move on to development once it's approved.

Download the brief

02. Structure Planning

At this stage, the logic of the future website takes shape:

  • the list of pages is defined;
  • the sequence of blocks is planned;
  • a convenient user journey is mapped out;
  • the placement of key elements is determined.

The main goal is to make the site not just attractive, but also clear, convenient, and logical for the visitor.

03. Gathering Materials

Before development begins, we gather all the materials the project needs. This helps get started faster and build a website that matches your expectations.

  • texts (if available);
  • photos, images, or other visual content;
  • logo and brand identity (if any);
  • examples of websites you like;
  • additional information about the company, services, or products.

04. Design Approval

Based on your preferences and references, a visual concept for the future website is formed: color palette, typography, interface style, and the project's overall mood.

Before development starts, I prepare a layout for us to approve together. Once approved, we move on to implementation.

05. Markup & Development

Once the design is approved, development begins using modern technologies: HTML, SCSS, and JavaScript.

This includes responsive markup, animations, interactive elements, and any functionality the project requires.

06. Device Adaptation

The website is checked and optimized for various devices and modern browsers:

  • smartphones;
  • tablets;
  • laptops;
  • large monitors.

Responsiveness is built in from the development stage, not added on after the project is finished.

07. Testing & Final Revisions

Before publishing, the project undergoes a comprehensive review:

  • correct display;
  • forms and links working properly;
  • loading speed;
  • compliance with the approved layout.

After reviewing it, you can provide a list of revisions within the agreed scope of work, after which the project is prepared for publishing.

08. Website Publishing

Once development is complete, the website is deployed to your chosen hosting and connected to your domain name.

If you haven't chosen a domain or hosting yet, I'll help you pick a suitable option and provide recommendations. Before publishing, basic SEO setup is performed: meta tags, page titles, favicon, Open Graph, and other parameters needed for the site to display and get indexed correctly.

09. Project Handover

Once the work is complete, you receive:

  • a link to the finished website;
  • the project's source files (if agreed upon in advance).

Further development, new changes, code-related training, or project support are not included in the development cost and are handled separately, by agreement. If needed, you can reach out to me or any other specialist.
